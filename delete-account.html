Delete Your KesMic Account

Your privacy matters. You can permanently delete your KesMic account and associated data at any time.
✓ In-App Account Deletion Available

How to Delete Your Account

KesMic allows you to permanently delete your account directly from within the mobile application.

  1. Open the KesMic: Invoice & Quote Maker mobile app.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Delete account.
  4. Type DELETE to confirm.
  5. Tap Delete my account.
Your account deletion request will permanently remove your KesMic account and associated business data. This action cannot be undone.
Download or share any invoice PDFs you need to keep before you continue. Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription bought through the App Store or Google Play. Cancel it in your store account settings to stop renewal.

Data That Will Be Deleted

  • User account information
  • Business profile details
  • Customers
  • Products and services
  • Invoices
  • Quotes
  • Payment records
  • Attachments and evidence photos
  • AI usage history
  • Application preferences

Data Retention

Some information may be retained where required by applicable laws, regulations, or legitimate business requirements such as financial record keeping obligations.
