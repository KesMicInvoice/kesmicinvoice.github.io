KesMic Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Introduction

KesMic ("we", "our", or "us") provides a mobile application that helps small businesses create invoices, quotes, manage customers, track payments, and organise business information.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you use the KesMic application.

2. Information We Collect

When you use KesMic, we may collect the following information:

  • Account information such as name, email address, and authentication details.
  • Business information such as business name, address, phone number, tax details, and logo.
  • Customer information that you choose to store, including names, email addresses, phone numbers, and addresses.
  • Invoice, quote, product, service, and payment information created within the app.
  • Photos and attachments added to invoices or quotes.
  • Voice input or text information provided when using AI invoice features.
  • Device and application information required to provide and improve the service.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Create and manage your account.
  • Provide invoice, quote, payment, and business management features.
  • Generate documents and PDFs.
  • Provide AI-powered invoice assistance.
  • Improve application performance and reliability.
  • Provide customer support.
  • Prevent fraud and misuse of our services.

4. AI Features

KesMic may use artificial intelligence features to help create invoices and extract information from user-provided text or voice input.

Information submitted through AI features is used only to provide the requested functionality. Users should avoid submitting unnecessary sensitive personal information through AI features.

5. Data Storage and Security

We use appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your information.

Data transmitted between the KesMic application and our servers is encrypted using secure connections.

While we take reasonable steps to protect your information, no online service can guarantee complete security.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

We may share information only when necessary to provide the service, such as with trusted technology providers that help operate our application, hosting services, authentication services, and AI processing services.

7. Account Deletion

You can request deletion of your KesMic account directly from within the application.

Deleting your account removes your profile and associated business data according to our deletion process.

For more information: Account Deletion

8. Your Responsibilities

You are responsible for ensuring that information you enter into KesMic, including customer and business information, is collected and used in accordance with applicable laws.

9. Children's Privacy

KesMic is designed for business users and is not intended for children under the applicable minimum age requirements.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
